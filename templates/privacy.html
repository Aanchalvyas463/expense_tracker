Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when using Spendly:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense details, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Record your expenses and show spending summaries.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service.
  • Respond to your questions and requests.

3. Data Storage

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google technical information such as your IP address when you visit. Google's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy.

We may also use service providers to host and operate Spendly, or share information where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at support@example.com.
